Privacy Policy

Galactic Void · Last updated November 25, 2026

Overview

Galactic Void is a free browser game. You can play without an account. Signing in with Google is optional and is used only to save your progress online and to power guilds and the friends list. We don't show ads, and we don't sell or share your information with anyone for marketing.

What we collect when you sign in

  • From your Google account: your name, email address and profile picture, plus an account ID. Your name and picture are shown to other players (for example in the friends list and guild roster). Your email address is never shown to other players.
  • Game data: your save file (character, progress, items and settings), and anything you do in guilds such as membership, rank, chat messages and items placed in the guild bank.
  • Online status: while you're playing, other signed-in players can see that you're online.

If you don't sign in, your progress stays in your own browser and nothing is sent to our servers.

Playing together

Co-op games connect players' browsers directly to each other. To set up that connection, your browser exchanges network details (including your IP address) with the other players in your squad and with the connection services listed below. Only join squads with people you choose to play with.

Services we use

  • Google — sign-in, and connection-helper (STUN) servers for co-op.
  • Supabase — stores accounts, save files and guild data.
  • Vercel — hosts the website.
  • PeerJS — helps players' browsers find each other for co-op.
  • Google Fonts — loads the game's fonts.

These services may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses and request times) under their own privacy policies.

How we use your information

Only to run the game: to sign you in, save and load your progress, show your name to friends and guildmates, and connect co-op games. We use no tracking or advertising cookies. The game stores your settings and sign-in session in your browser's local storage.

Your choices

  • You can play without signing in at any time, and you can sign out from the title screen.
  • Erase All Saves in Settings deletes your save, including your online copy.
  • To delete your account and all data tied to it, contact the developer at the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for Galactic Void, and it will be removed.
  • You can also remove the game's access to your Google account at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

Galactic Void is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
